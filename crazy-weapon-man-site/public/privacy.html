Privacy

Crazy Weapon Man is a free browser game. It has no accounts, no ads, no trackers and no third-party scripts.

Stored in your browser

Sound settings, weapon mastery, secrets found and speedrun times are saved in your browser's local storage so your progress survives a reload. Clearing your site data removes them.

Sent to us

Nothing. The game doesn't send your name, scores or any other information about you to a server. If a public leaderboard is added later, this page will say exactly what it sends before it goes live.

Hosting

The game is hosted on Cloudflare, which keeps standard request logs to run its network.
